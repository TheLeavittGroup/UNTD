NEXT STEPS

What happens after you ask for numbers.

  1. Send me a recent utility bill or your average monthly bill.
  2. I check the home, utility, roof layout, and qualification details.
  3. You review payment, production, batteries, and utility bill expectations.
  4. If the numbers make sense, the project moves into survey, design, permits, install, inspection, and utility approval.
