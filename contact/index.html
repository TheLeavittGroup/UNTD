PROGRAM INFO

What homeowners usually want to know first.

Simple, homeowner-safe points without rep tools, logins, or internal links.

$0 down options

Many programs are structured with no upfront down payment, depending on approval and final terms.

Solar + battery payment

The goal is to compare your current utility path against a solar or solar-plus-battery payment that is easier to understand long term.

Utility bill may remain

You may still receive a utility bill for connection charges, taxes, minimums, or energy not fully covered by the system.

Battery value

Batteries can help store energy, reduce peak-hour exposure, and may provide backup value depending on equipment and utility rules.

Site survey + design

After initial approval, the home moves through survey, design, permits, install, inspection, and utility approval.

NEXT STEPS

What happens after you ask for numbers.

A simple overview so the process is clear from the start.

  1. Send me a recent utility bill or your average monthly bill.
  2. I check the home, utility, roof layout, and basic qualification details.
  3. You review estimated payment, production, battery options, and utility bill expectations.
  4. If you like the numbers, the project moves into survey, design, permits, install, inspection, and utility approval.
