The Power of Net Metering.

Sell Energy Back
to the Grid

Once solar is installed the customer will sell energy back to the utility company when generating more than the home requires. During this time the meter will spin backwards showing a negative consumption of energy.

Earn Energy Credits
to Offset Energy Use

As energy is sold to the utility company the customer earns energy credits that can be used during low solar production periods or peak times of the year.
