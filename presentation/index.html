Before moving forward, we review the priorities, the numbers, and the simple next step.

“I need to think about it.”

That makes sense. Usually that means the numbers, the process, or the timing still needs to be clearer. We can review whichever one matters most.

“I want to compare options.”

That is fair. The important part is comparing payment, yearly adjustment, production, battery, installer, warranty, and what the utility bill looks like after solar.

“What if I move?”

Then the next buyer is looking at a home with a different monthly energy picture. We can review how that transfer process works.

“I don’t like long-term agreements.”

That is fair. The utility path is also long-term, but without much control over future pricing. This is about choosing which path gives the home more predictability.
