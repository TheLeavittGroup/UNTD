Data Center Warning

WHAT IID SAYS COULD GO WRONG

IID is receiving increased interest from massive data-center developers. The district’s own public warning lists risks to electricity costs, grid reliability, water supplies and existing ratepayers.

100+ MW

Power Demand Like a Small City

IID says many new data centers can demand upwards of 100 megawatts each—equivalent to powering a small city.

24/7

Constant High-Volume Load

Servers and cooling systems operate around the clock and require stable, high-volume electricity.

GRID STRESS

Reliability Could Be Pressured

IID warns that sudden or large load additions may pressure the reliability of its core electric system.

RATEPAYER RISK

The Community Could Be Exposed

IID says that if major infrastructure becomes underused or abandoned, its cost could fall on the community.

STRANDED ASSETS

Expensive Equipment Could Be Left Behind

Substations and power lines built for a project that fails or scales down could leave existing ratepayers exposed.

WATER + LAND

Huge Physical Footprint

IID says cooling can require significant water and a single site can occupy 40–80 acres or more.
